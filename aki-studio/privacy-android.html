Aki Studio for Android · Updated 16 October 2026

Privacy policy

Android release preparation. The Android app has not been published on Google Play. This policy describes the current Android release candidate.

Aki provides Aki Studio. This policy covers the Android Official (store) edition and the separate Android personal edition. The iOS app has a separate policy. For privacy questions, contact akistudio.mobile@gmail.com.

Your photographs and projects

Aki Studio reads the photographs you choose through Android's image or file picker. Imported originals, including any metadata, previews, editing instructions, imported Looks and saved projects stay in local app storage. Processing happens on your device. Aki Studio does not upload this content to an Aki server or send it to Google's advertising SDK.

The app has no user account, hosted photo library, project synchronization or separate publisher analytics service. Edits are kept separately from the original; exporting creates a finished copy. All included Looks and editing tools are free, without a subscription or in-app purchase.

Saving, sharing and permissions

You choose whether to save a finished image to your gallery or share it with another app. Older Android versions may request storage permission to save a copy. A file or photo provider may retrieve a cloud-backed image when you select it; that provider handles the transfer under its own settings and policy.

Exports are separate copies. A gallery, file provider or app you choose may synchronize or upload them according to your settings and its policies. Aki Studio's local storage and backup settings do not control those services.

Official edition: ads and export

The Android Official edition includes Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). Its release configuration uses production advertising. The separate personal edition contains neither SDK and does not show ads or advertising consent forms.

The Watch ad to export action may show a rewarded ad before saving or sharing. Completing or skipping the ad allows the same image quality after it closes, while that export attempt remains active. If an ad is unavailable or fails, the current eligible export continues without it. There is no cash reward, transferable credit or account balance.

Leaving the app, cancelling the export or an incompatible open native dialog can end that attempt; your originals and edits remain on your device. You can return and start a new export. No ad is automatically shown after a successful export.

After its consent checks allow ad requests, the app can prepare an ad before you tap export, including while you edit. Google's advertising services can therefore receive advertising-related information before an export. Your photographs, their metadata and editing instructions are not part of the app's ad requests.

Information used by Google

Google's advertising SDK may collect and share:

  • IP address and approximate location;
  • app and ad activity, including launches, taps and video views;
  • performance and diagnostic information;
  • Android advertising ID, app set ID and applicable device or account identifiers.

Google uses this information for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention, and says its SDK encrypts it in transit using TLS. Read Google's Android advertising data disclosure and Google's privacy policy for its practices, retention and controls.

Non-personalized advertising

Aki Studio requests non-personalized ads and disables Google's publisher personalization and publisher first-party ID settings. These choices do not make ads anonymous or stop all SDK identifiers, network requests or data processing. Google's privacy policy and applicable choices still govern its advertising services.

Consent and advertising choices

The Official edition requests an updated consent status when it starts and presents Google's form when required and available. Ad requests proceed only when Google's consent SDK permits them. Available messages and choices depend on your region, the messages configured for the app and your choices.

Open Privacy → Ad privacy choices when that option is available to revisit Google's privacy options. Android's advertising settings also offer advertising-ID controls. You do not need to agree to personalized advertising to keep editing or exporting. If your choices prevent an eligible ad from loading, export can proceed without an ad. An open consent or privacy dialog may need to finish before a new export starts.

Local retention and deletion

Saved projects remain until you delete them or remove the app's data. Photo copies still used by another project or current undo history may remain. Clearing Aki Studio's storage in Android Settings or uninstalling it removes local app data as managed by Android. Export anything you need first: a finished image is not a backup of an editable project.

The Android app's backup rules exclude its private app data from cloud backup and device transfer. Aki provides no hosted project backup. Exports in your gallery, files or another service remain until you delete them there; deleting a project does not remove those separate copies.

Google manages advertising and consent records under its own policies and controls. Removing Aki Studio's data does not ask Aki to erase Google's records.

Support and privacy requests

If you email akistudio.mobile@gmail.com, Aki and our email provider receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. We use them to respond and troubleshoot your request. Send only the details needed and use a non-sensitive sample if an image is necessary.

Support correspondence is kept as needed to handle the request and related obligations. Contact the same address to ask about access, correction or deletion of information you sent, or with a parent or guardian's privacy concern. Available rights depend on your location and the information involved. Aki cannot remotely retrieve or erase projects stored only on your device.

Changes and contact
