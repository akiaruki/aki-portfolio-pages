A few useful details

Before you
step inside.

Is Aki Studio available now?+

The iOS beta is available through TestFlight until October 15, 2026. Android is coming soon. Aki Studio is still in beta; this is not a production App Store release.

Can I edit RAW photographs?+

Aki Studio supports RAW workflows, with compatibility depending on your camera, file format and device. Support is not universal across every camera or RAW file.

Can I bring my own looks?+

You can import supported creative .cube LUTs made for SDR/sRGB, and use .aki adaptive packages. Other LUT types or color pipelines may not be supported.

Where can I share feedback?+

Tell us what you’re making, what feels right, and what could work better. Write to akistudio.mobile@gmail.com.
