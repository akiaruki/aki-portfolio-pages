step inside.

Is Aki Studio available now?+

You can try Aki Studio on iPhone and iPad through TestFlight now. The public App Store release is under review. Android is coming soon.

How do I install with TestFlight?+

Follow the installation steps. Install Apple's TestFlight app first, then return here to open Studio's public invitation. No redemption code is needed. Returning testers may see Update or Open instead of Install.

Is everything free?+

Yes. Every built-in Look and all twelve editing tools are free, with no subscriptions or in-app purchases. The public release is planned to be supported by ads.

When will I see ads?+

An ad may appear after an edited copy has been successfully saved. Ads are spaced apart, so you won't see one after every save. Ad availability and appearance may differ by installed build and region. Read our privacy policy for more details.

Can I edit RAW photographs?+

Aki Studio supports RAW workflows, with compatibility depending on the camera, file format and device. Support is not universal across every camera or RAW file.

Can I bring my own looks?+

You can import supported creative .cube LUTs made for SDR/sRGB, and use .aki adaptive packages. Other LUT types or color pipelines may not be supported.

Where can I get help or share feedback?+

Visit Aki Studio support or write to akistudio.mobile@gmail.com.

A few taps to get started.

  1. Download TestFlight from the App Store on your iPhone or iPad.
  2. Open TestFlight. If it asks for an invitation or redemption code, leave it blank.
  3. Close TestFlight and return to this webpage.
  4. Tap Open in TestFlight to open Studio's invitation.
  5. In TestFlight, tap Accept, then Install to download Studio.

No redemption code is needed for this public link. If you already joined, TestFlight may show Update or Open instead.

TestFlight access depends on available spaces and device compatibility.
