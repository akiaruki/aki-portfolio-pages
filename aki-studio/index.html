A few useful details

Before you
step inside.

Is Aki Studio available now?+

You can try Aki Studio on iPhone and iPad through TestFlight now. The public App Store release is coming soon. Android is coming soon.

Is everything free?+

Yes. Every built-in Look and all twelve editing tools are free, with no subscriptions or in-app purchases. The public release is planned to be supported by ads.

When will I see ads?+

An ad may appear after your photograph has successfully saved to Photos. Ads are spaced apart, so you won’t see one after every save. The current TestFlight version uses Google test ads. Read our privacy policy for more details.

Can I edit RAW photographs?+

Aki Studio supports RAW workflows, with compatibility depending on the camera, file format and device. Support is not universal across every camera or RAW file.

Can I bring my own looks?+

You can import supported creative .cube LUTs made for SDR/sRGB, and use .aki adaptive packages. Other LUT types or color pipelines may not be supported.

Where can I get help or share feedback?+

Visit Aki Studio support or write to akistudio.mobile@gmail.com.

About Aki Studio

A point of view.
Room for yours.

We believe an edit should serve the photograph: its light, its color, and the feeling worth keeping. Aki Studio brings that idea to a compact mobile workspace, with expressive starting points and familiar tools you can make your own.

Your photographs and editing instructions are processed on your device. You choose what to import, how far to take an edit, and when to save a copy.
