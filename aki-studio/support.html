A few useful answers

Are all Looks and tools free?

Yes. All Looks and editing tools are free, with no subscriptions, in-app purchases, or paid feature tiers.

Where are my photos and projects stored?

Editing happens on your device. Projects stay in the app's local storage, and exported copies go to the Photos or Files location you choose. Aki Studio has no cloud project backup, so keep a separate copy of important photographs.

What if a photo won't import or save?

Check the app's Photos permission in your device's Settings and make sure there is space available on your device. If the issue continues, tell us the file format, whether you used Photos or Files, and any message shown in the app.

Why did an ad appear after saving?

Aki Studio may show an ad after confirming that an edited copy was saved to Photos. Ads are spaced at least three minutes apart, with a limit of three per hour. If an ad is unavailable, your save still completes.

The current TestFlight build uses Google test ads. Close them using the ad's close or dismiss control; you do not need to open the advertised content or install anything.

How can I review advertising privacy choices?

Where available, use the app's advertising privacy options. Choices may vary by region and the messages available for the app. Our Privacy Policy explains the information Google services may handle.

How do I report a beta issue?

You can send feedback through TestFlight or email support. Include the version and build shown in TestFlight, what you expected, and what happened instead. Please check Photos for the saved copy before repeating a save after an ad or unexpected interruption.
