The capture

A little instinct.
A little intention.

Stay with automatic capture, or take a more considered approach. Camera puts framing and supported camera controls within reach.

FRAME

Give the moment its shape.

Choose from nine aspect ratios, from square and portrait to wide cinematic framing. Pinch to zoom and settle on your composition.

FOCUS

Choose what matters.

Tap to focus, or hold to lock focus and exposure. Set a 3- or 10-second photo timer when you need a moment before the shutter.

CONTROL

Follow the light.

Adjust exposure, ISO, shutter speed, white balance and focus where your selected camera supports them.

Available controls, video resolutions and frame rates depend on your device and selected camera.

A few useful details

Before you
step outside.

Can I download Aki Camera now?

Camera is coming soon. A public App Store or TestFlight download is not available from this page yet.

Can I use Looks for video too?

Yes. Camera lets you preview your chosen Look while composing and apply it to photos and videos. Video options vary by device and selected camera.

Can I import my own LUTs?

Camera supports compatible photographic sRGB .cube LUTs. Compatibility depends on the file and its intended color workflow.

What happens when I transfer a capture?

A successful transfer places the selected file in Apple Photos and removes the corresponding copy from App’s Gallery.

Where can I ask a question?

Email akistudio.mobile@gmail.com for questions about Aki Camera. Include your device and what you would like help with.
