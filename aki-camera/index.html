The light / A more considered capture

Let instinct lead.
Take control
when you want to.

Automatic capture keeps things simple. When the scene calls for more intention, adjust the controls supported by your device and selected camera.

Hardware matters. Control availability can differ between devices and cameras; some settings may not be available in every capture mode.

Exposure
Guide the overall brightness as the light changes.
ISO & shutter
Adjust sensitivity and shutter speed where your selected camera supports them.
White balance
Take a more deliberate approach to the warmth or coolness of the scene.
Focus
Tap to focus, or adjust focus manually where supported.
Focus & exposure lock
Hold to lock focus and exposure when you want to keep your chosen point steady.
Zoom
Pinch to find your framing with the camera you have selected.

A few useful details

Before you
step outside.

Can I download Aki Camera now?

Public beta access is being prepared. This page will be updated with a public invitation or store link when one is available. The App Store release is coming soon.

Are these artworks photographs made with the Looks?

No. These are the original illustrated artworks used to identify the Looks inside Camera. They communicate each Look’s character. They are not sample capture results or before-and-after photographs.

Are there 40 Looks, or 41?

There are 40 built-in Looks across Daily, B&W, Aki, Film and Cinematic. Original is a separate, unfiltered option and is not counted among the forty.

Can I use Looks for video too?

Yes. Preview your chosen Look while composing and apply it to photographs and videos. Available video options depend on your device and selected camera.

Can I import my own LUTs?

Camera supports compatible photographic sRGB .cube LUTs. Compatibility depends on the file and its intended color workflow.

Do all devices have the same manual controls?

No. Exposure, ISO, shutter speed, white balance, focus, video resolution and frame-rate options depend on the device and selected camera.

What happens when I transfer a capture?

A successful transfer places the selected file in Apple Photos and removes the corresponding copy from Camera’s gallery.

Does Camera include advertising?

Yes. Ads may appear after a successful transfer to Apple Photos.

Where can I ask a question?

Email akistudio.mobile@gmail.com for help with Aki Camera. Include your device model and the details of what you would like help with.
