01 / FIND THE SHAPE OF YOUR STORY

A different page.
The same point of view.

Start with one photograph or bring together as many as nine. Explore 66 layouts across quiet editorial, bold graphic, gallery and cinematic collections. Choose a direction, then make it your own.

  • Beyond the borderEight Borderless designs let your photographs fill the page.
  • A fresh angleSix Angular designs frame images through slanted and geometric windows.
  • Room for your wordsEight typography-led designs reserve clear space for headlines and supporting text.
02 / GIVE YOUR WORDS A VOICE

Type with
personality.

Choose from 20 type presets. Set a headline, write a caption, add a quote or build a longer passage. Alignment, paragraph controls and text rotation help every word find its place.

Up to eight extra text blocks, alongside your headline, subtitle, label and caption.

03 / COMPOSE YOUR WAY

Move it.
Make it yours.

Arrange photo frames, fine-tune a crop, and move text and swatches around your page. Finish with circular, square, rounded-square or diamond color details.

Five formats for your different stories. Symmetry supports even photo counts from two to eight.

04 / KEEP CREATING

From your device.
To wherever next.

Keep editable projects on your device and return to them later. Export a finished PNG or JPEG to Photos, Files or the system share sheet.

The editor and fonts are bundled in the app. An export ad may appear after you choose Watch ad to export; an unavailable or dismissed ad still allows export. Advertising and consent use the internet.
