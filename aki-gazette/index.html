02 / Give your words a voice

Type with
personality.

Go from a quiet serif to a bold headline. Explore expressive type pairings, from Bodoni Moda, Cormorant Garamond and Newsreader to Fraunces, Barlow Condensed and Syne. Caveat, Kalam and Patrick Hand add a handwritten touch.

Let a typography-led layout suggest its pairing, or choose your own. The fonts are included in the app, ready when inspiration arrives.

  • A little or a lot. Write a headline, supporting copy, label and caption, then add up to eight extra text blocks.
  • Words with structure. Add a kicker, byline, quote, body passage or custom note, with line breaks and paragraph controls.
  • Room to write. In Preview, tap text to select it, then tap again. An enlarged editing window opens above your usual keyboard while the main preview keeps its size.
  • Four alignments, within reach. Choose left, centre, right or justify while typing. Tap outside or choose Done to apply your words and alignment.
03 / Compose your way

The starting point
is yours to change.

A layout gets the page moving. Preview changes photographs inside their frames. Adjust brings compact tools to selected frames, text, shapes and colour swatches, keeping the page in place.

Keep the small things in view

In Adjust, select a frame, text block, shape or swatch group. Drag from elsewhere on the preview to move it, keeping your fingers out of the way.

Pinch. Twist. Place.

Pinch outside selected text to scale the lettering. Pinch inside it or drag its side handles to change wrapping width. Alignment guides and angle snapping help items settle into place.

Find the crop

In Preview, drag a photograph inside its frame and pinch to zoom. In Adjust, choose a fixed or freeform frame ratio; inside gestures adjust the crop, while outside gestures move and resize the frame.

Cleaner panels. A simple tap.

Panels leave out the close button. Tap outside to dismiss them and return to your composition. Item icons open compact tools for font, colour, opacity and blending.

Glass, colour and smoother movement

Add Solid or Glass shapes, adjust tint and blur, and explore sixteen blend modes. Use side handles to change a shape’s width and top or bottom handles to change its height. Pinch to scale and rotate the whole shape.

Make space for another idea

In Adjust, hold an item to reveal Duplicate. Open Layers, hold anywhere on an item’s row, then drag to change its order. A quick swipe scrolls the list.

06 / Send your story into the world

From your device.
To wherever next.

Choose PNG for crisp detail or JPEG for a familiar, shareable image. For screen formats, choose a width of 1080, 1440 or 2160 pixels. Gazette shows the full dimensions for your chosen format: a Story at 2160 pixels wide is 2160 × 3840. A4 exports at 2480 × 3508 and 4R at 1200 × 1800, both with 300 dpi metadata.

Save the finished composition to Photos, choose a place in Files or send it through the system share sheet.

  • Free, with an ad at export. Choose Watch ad to export when your image is ready. There is one ad opportunity for that export.
  • Keep saving. A skipped, dismissed or unavailable ad still allows export. You can cancel the export yourself when you want to return to editing.
  • Keep your originals. Gazette creates a separate finished image. It does not overwrite the photographs you imported.
07 / Keep creating

A place to return to.

Keep editable projects on your device and return to their photographs, text and layout later. Export a finished image when you are ready to share it.

PNG and JPEG flatten the composition into an image. Keep the local project if you want to change individual elements again.

Projects stay on your device.

Gazette does not require an account or upload your project photographs and text to an Aki server. The editor, layouts and fonts are bundled in the app.

Local projects are not a cloud backup. Deleting the app or clearing its data can remove them, so save finished images somewhere you want to keep them.

Know what connects.

Advertising and consent services use the internet and may process device, advertising, usage and diagnostic data. Gazette requests nonpersonalized ads.

Permission to add an export to Photos is separate from selecting images to import. Files and sharing use the system destination you choose.

Read the privacy policy for details about local data, permissions and Google’s advertising services. For practical help with projects and saving, visit Gazette support.

Before you begin

A few useful answers.

Start with the editing guide, or visit support for help.

What is Aki Gazette for?

Gazette combines photographs, text, shapes and colour into a single composition. Create photo stories and portfolios, or make graphics for reflections, announcements and promotions. Start with a design, then make it your own.

Which devices can use Gazette through TestFlight?

Gazette supports iPhone with iOS 16 or later and iPad with iPadOS 16 or later. When an approved build is available, open the TestFlight invitation on your device and follow its installation steps. See the release availability note above for the latest update.

How do I install Gazette through TestFlight?

When an approved build is available, install Apple’s TestFlight app, then return here and open Gazette’s public invitation. New testers tap Accept, then Install. You don’t need a redemption code. The latest build is waiting for TestFlight review; the public invitation shows current availability. Follow the step-by-step installation guide above.

Is Gazette available on Android?

The Android build is prepared. A public Google Play download is not available yet. The latest iPhone and iPad update is awaiting App Store and TestFlight review.

How do I arrange a small item without covering it?

In Adjust, tap the item to select it. Drag from another part of the preview to move it, or pinch and twist there with two fingers to resize and rotate. Tap the selected item again to deselect it, or tap another item to switch. The Adjust guide walks through the controls.

Is Gazette free?

Yes. Gazette is free with export ads. You choose Watch ad to export to begin saving a finished composition. An ad that is skipped, dismissed or unavailable does not prevent the export.

How many photos can I use?

A composition supports one to nine photographs. Layouts adapt to the number you choose. The optional Symmetry setting is available for even counts of two, four, six or eight photographs.

What happens when I type?

A larger text editor opens above the usual keyboard, and the main preview keeps its size. Left, centre, right and justify remain visible while you type. Tap outside or choose Done to apply the edit. These controls are shown in the version 1.2.1 screenshots.

Can I add more than a headline?

Yes. Headline, supporting copy, label and caption fields are joined by up to eight extra text blocks. Choose a kicker, byline, quote, body passage or custom note, then adjust its alignment, position and rotation.

Can I use Gazette without an account or connection?

No account is required. The editor, templates and fonts are included in the app, and projects are stored locally. Advertising and consent need a connection. If an export ad is unavailable, Gazette still lets you continue saving.

Will it change my original photographs?

No. Cropping, positioning and composition changes happen inside Gazette’s project. Export creates a separate finished image. Saving to Photos requires permission to add that exported image; choosing Files or Share uses the system destination you select.

Can I reopen an exported image as an editable project?

A PNG or JPEG is a flattened image, so it does not keep separate editable frames and text. Reopen the saved project in Gazette to continue editing. Projects remain on that device and are not synced through an Aki account.

How can I get help or share feedback?

Send feedback from Gazette’s page in TestFlight, visit Gazette support or email akistudio.mobile@gmail.com. Include your device, iOS version, Gazette build and what you were trying to do. Send only photographs or screenshots you are comfortable sharing.
