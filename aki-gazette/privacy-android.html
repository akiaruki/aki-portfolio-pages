Privacy policy

Android release preparation. The Android app has not been published on Google Play. This policy describes the current Android release candidate.

Aki provides Aki Gazette. This policy covers the Android Official, ad-supported edition. The iPhone and iPad app has a separate policy. For privacy questions, contact akistudio.mobile@gmail.com.

Your photographs, text and projects

Gazette reads images you choose through Android's file picker. Copies of the original files, including any metadata, project names, entered text, crops, colours, layouts and editable projects are stored locally in the app. Editing, sampling, previews and export rendering happen on your device. Gazette does not upload your photos or design text to an Aki server or send them to the advertising SDK.

The app has no user account, hosted project library, project synchronization or separate publisher analytics service. Bundled designs, writing and stickers are available locally; there is no public user-posting feed. A file provider may retrieve a cloud-backed image you select, under that provider's own settings and policy.

Exports and permissions

You choose where each PNG or JPEG is saved through Android's file destination chooser, or share a copy with another app where that action is offered. Gazette uses the file picker for the images you select; it does not request precise location permission.

An export is a separate finished image. A destination app or file provider handles your selected copy under its own policies and may synchronize it according to your settings. Deleting a Gazette project does not delete copies in your files, gallery or another service.

Advertising and export

The Android Official edition includes Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). The Watch ad to export action may show a rewarded ad before the destination chooser. Completing or dismissing the ad still permits the same export quality while the attempt remains active. An unavailable ad or an ad failing to load or display does not remove the export tool. No cash, transferable credit or account balance is offered.

Cancelling the export itself, leaving the app or losing an active handoff can end that attempt without changing your open composition. You can return and start a new export. The app does not automatically present an ad after a successful export.

When its consent checks allow advertising, Gazette can preload an ad before you tap export, including while you edit. Google's services can therefore receive advertising-related information before an export. Your photographs, design text and layout instructions are not included in Gazette's ad requests.

Information used by Google

Google's advertising SDK may collect and share:

  • IP address and approximate location;
  • app and ad activity, including launches, taps and video views;
  • performance and diagnostic information;
  • Android advertising ID, app set ID and applicable device or account identifiers.

Google uses this information for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention, and says its SDK encrypts it in transit using TLS. Read Google's Android advertising data disclosure and Google's privacy policy for its practices, retention and controls.

Advertising personalization

The current Android Official edition uses Google's default ad-request and publisher first-party ID settings. It does not force every request to be non-personalized or switch off publisher first-party IDs. Ads may be personalized where the applicable privacy signals, your choices and Google's settings allow it. Other requests may receive non-personalized or limited ads, or no ad.

Google explains its advertising personalization settings and publisher first-party ID. This Android policy does not make the separate iOS app's non-personalized-ad promise for the Android edition.

Consent and advertising choices

Gazette requests updated consent information when it starts and presents Google's form when required and available. Ad requests proceed only when Google's consent SDK permits them. Available messages and choices depend on your region, the messages configured for the app and your choices.

Open About → Privacy → Advertising choices when the option is available to reopen Google's privacy options. Android's advertising settings also offer advertising-ID controls. Editing remains available. If the SDK does not permit an ad request or no ad can be shown, export can continue without an ad; a currently open consent/privacy dialog may need to finish before a new export.

Keeping and removing local data

Saved projects, project backups and settings remain in the app's local storage until you remove them or remove app data. Deleting a project removes its project record and associated backup. Photo copies still referenced by another project, another backup, current undo history or saved custom stickers may remain.

Clear Gazette's storage in Android Settings or uninstall it to remove its local data as managed by Android. Export anything you need first; a finished PNG or JPEG is not an editable-project backup. The Android app's backup rules exclude private app data from cloud backup and device transfer. Aki provides no hosted project backup.

Exports stay in their chosen destination until you delete them there. Google manages its advertising and consent records under its own policies and controls; Aki cannot erase those records through this app.

Support and privacy requests

If you email akistudio.mobile@gmail.com, Aki and our email provider receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. We use them to respond and troubleshoot your request. Send only the details needed and use a non-sensitive sample if an image is necessary.

Support correspondence is kept as needed to handle the request and related obligations. Contact the same address to ask about access, correction or deletion of information you sent, or with a parent or guardian's privacy concern. Available rights depend on your location and the information involved. Aki cannot remotely retrieve or erase projects stored only on your device.

GitHub may process technical request data, including IP addresses and browser or request information, to operate its hosting services. See GitHub's privacy statement. External links and email use their destination service under its own policies.

Changes and contact

We will update the date when this policy changes. For privacy questions or requests, contact Aki at akistudio.mobile@gmail.com.
