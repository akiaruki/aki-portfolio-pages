Gazette support

Questions about your compositions, local projects or exports? We’re here to help.

Include your device model, iOS version, Gazette version and the steps that led to the issue. A screenshot can help; leave out private photographs and sensitive information. We do not need your Apple password.

Start with your photographs

Tap Start with your photos and choose 1–9 images. Pick a layout and format, add your words, and adjust frames, crops and color swatches. Gazette has 66 layouts and 20 type presets. The Symmetry layout uses even photo counts from 2–8.

Where are my projects?

Use Saved projects on the home screen to reopen work saved on that device. Gazette has no sign-in or project cloud-sync service. Projects are held in local app storage; they do not automatically appear on another device.

Before deleting the app, export compositions you want to keep. PNG and JPEG files are finished images and cannot restore an editable project. If storage is full, free space elsewhere first and retry without deleting Gazette’s data.

How do I export?

Tap Export, choose PNG or JPEG and a resolution, then select Watch ad to export. An available rewarded ad may appear. Once it is dismissed, choose Save to Photos, Save to Files or Share…. If an ad cannot load or display, export remains available. Completing an ad is not required to keep or export your composition.

Gazette is free with export ads. Debug and TestFlight builds use Google test rewarded ads. Editing resources and fonts are bundled; advertising and consent need a network connection. Please do not repeatedly tap or interact with live ads just to test them.

An export did not finish

If you cancelled a chooser or left the app before the save handoff, return and tap export again. Your open composition is unchanged. Check free storage; for a very large image, try a smaller export resolution.

If Photos permission was denied, try Files or Share. You can also review Gazette’s Photos permission in iOS Settings. A successful Files or Share operation saves to the destination you selected, which may have its own account or connectivity requirements.

How do I change ad privacy choices?

Open About → Privacy. When Google makes privacy options available for your region and consent state, Advertising choices appears there. If it is unavailable or a form fails to load, you can still edit and export. Read the Gazette privacy policy for data handling and provider information.
