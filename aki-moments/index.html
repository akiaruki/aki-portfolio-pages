01 / Start with a story

A little room
to make it yours.

From choosing a canvas to arranging your final cut, Moments brings your video, photos, words and sound together in one place.

01 / Begin

Choose your canvas.

Start with a frame that fits your story. Choose a familiar aspect ratio or a stacked format for multiple views.

02 / Arrange

Make the cut.

Bring in clips and photos, split them on the timeline and move pieces into place.

03 / Finish

Find the feeling.

Add text, animated chalk, audio and transitions. Explore Looks and Vibes, then choose the quality and frame rate for your export.
