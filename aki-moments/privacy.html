Privacy Policy

Aki Studio makes Moments, a video editing app. This policy covers the Android and iOS apps and support requests. For privacy questions, contact akistudio.mobile@gmail.com.

Your projects and media

Moments processes the videos, photos, audio, text, and editing instructions you choose on your device. Your projects and imported copies are stored in the app on your device. We do not operate a Moments server that receives your projects or provides cloud backup. Your device's backup settings and any file or cloud providers you choose may make copies of app data or media. Exports are saved separately, such as to Photos or a destination chosen through the share sheet; your device, cloud provider, or other apps may handle those files according to your settings and their own policies.

On iOS, you can choose media to import and may allow Moments to add finished videos to Photos. You can also share an exported video using the system share sheet; the destination you choose handles the shared file under its own practices.

Advertising and privacy choices

The public edition uses Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). Moments may show a rewarded ad when you choose to save an export; when an ad is unavailable or dismissed, the app can continue saving. Google and its partners may process device and advertising identifiers, IP address and approximate location derived from it, app and ad interactions, diagnostics, and related technical information to deliver and measure advertising, prevent fraud, and operate their services. The information processed depends on device settings, region, and your choices. Google explains its practices in Google's Privacy Policy.

On iOS, Moments configures its ad requests as non-personalized. This does not mean Google's SDK or its partners process no data. Google may show a privacy message where required. When available, use the Privacy or Privacy choices control on the home screen to review or change your choices. You can also manage advertising and app permissions in your Android or iOS device settings.

Retention and deletion

You can delete projects within Moments and uninstall the app to remove its app-private data from the device. Exported files and originals stored elsewhere remain until you delete them there, and backup copies may remain according to your device and provider settings. Google retains and deletes information it receives according to its own policies and settings. If you contact us, we and our email provider receive your message, email address, and any attachments you provide; we use them to respond and keep them as needed to handle the request. Ask us to delete a support message by emailing akistudio.mobile@gmail.com.

Security and changes

Moments stores app-private project data using the storage controls provided by Android or iOS. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure. We may update this policy when features or practices change; the date above shows the latest revision.
